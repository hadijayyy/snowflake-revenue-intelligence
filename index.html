Keep the interpretation honest.

  • The near-term amounts are derived from rounded management percentages. The two forward 12-month windows differ; this is not a fixed-cohort comparison.
  • RPO includes revenue expected across future periods. Contract timing, duration, foreign exchange, and usage can affect it. A lower balance alone does not prove weaker demand.
  • Net revenue retention measures trailing revenue expansion, not the percentage of customers retained. The $1M+ count uses trailing annual product revenue.
  • The $1.64B upside is a scenario assumption, not a statistically validated forecast. No model accuracy claim is made here.
  • Account-level usage, renewal exposure, and spending plans are not available in these public disclosures.
