# Metric definitions

| Metric | Definition | Unit | Interpretation limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product revenue | Product revenue recognized during the quarter | USD M in inputs; USD B in charts | Separate from total revenue and remaining contracts |
| Remaining performance obligations (RPO) | Contracted future revenue not yet recognized | USD M | Not bookings, pipeline, cash, or next-quarter revenue |
| Next-12-month share | Management's approximate RPO share expected in the following 12 months | Percent | Rounded and forward-looking; the horizon advances |
| Implied next-12-month RPO | Total RPO × rounded recognition share | USD M | Analyst calculation; not a fixed cohort or exact reported growth series |
| Net revenue retention (NRR) | Trailing product revenue from the measured existing customer cohort relative to that cohort's prior-period revenue | Percent | 126% means 26% revenue expansion for the measured cohort, not 126% of customers retained |
| $1M+ customers | Customers generating more than $1M in trailing 12-month product revenue | Customers | Does not establish concentration, profitability, or account risk |
| GAAP operating margin | GAAP operating income or loss / total revenue | Percent | Q2 FY27: −17.0% |
| Adjusted operating margin | Management's non-GAAP operating income / total revenue | Percent | Q2 FY27: 15.3%; examine reconciliation and exclusions |
| Sequential growth | (current quarter / prior quarter − 1) × 100 | Percent | Seasonality requires context |
| Year-over-year growth | (current quarter / same quarter last year − 1) × 100 | Percent | Historical comparison, not a forecast |
| Q1 index | Value relative to Q1 FY27 × 100 | Index | Retained in SQL; not used in the primary report |

Definitions follow the [earnings release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1640147/000164014726000033/fy2027q2earnings.htm) and [presentation](https://investors.snowflake.com/files/doc_financials/2027/q2/Q2-FY2027-Investor-Presentation_vF.pdf). Consult the originals when extending the analysis.
