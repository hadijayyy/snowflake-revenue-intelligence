# Metric definitions

| Metric | Definition used here | Unit | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product revenue | Product revenue recognized during the reported quarter | USD millions | What the company earned in the quarter |
| Contracted future revenue | Future revenue already under contract | USD millions | Size of the committed future base |
| Net revenue retention | Revenue retained and expanded from existing customers | Percent | Whether the existing customer base is growing or shrinking |
| $1M+ customers | Customers with more than $1M in trailing product revenue | Customers | Scale of the large-customer segment |
| Sequential growth | `(current quarter / previous quarter) - 1` | Percent | Direction versus the immediately prior quarter |
| Year-over-year growth | `(current quarter / same quarter prior year) - 1` | Percent | Direction versus the comparable prior-year quarter |
| Q1 index | Q1 FY27 value set to 100 | Index | Baseline for directional comparison only |

## Interpretation guardrails

- Product revenue is recognized revenue; contracted future revenue is a forward-looking contracted base.
- Net revenue retention is a trailing customer metric and can confirm momentum with a delay.
- The $1M+ customer count is a scale indicator, not a measure of profitability or customer quality.
- A sequential decline in contracted future revenue is a signal to investigate, not proof that future growth will decline.
